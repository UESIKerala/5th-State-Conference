Venue & Navigation Guide:

Christian Education Center, Kuriannoor Thiruvalla is located atop Charalkunnu Mount in Kuriannoor, near Kozhencherry / Thiruvalla, Pathanamthitta District, Kerala.
Exact Address: Charal Mount Rd, Kolabhagom PO, Kuriannoor, Thiruvalla, Thottapuzhassery, Kerala 689545

Chengannur Railway Station (CNGR) – ~16 km from venue
Tiruvalla Railway Station (TRVL) – ~18 km from venue

Last-Mile Transport: Take a KSRTC or private bus heading toward Kozhencherry/Pamba from the Thiruvalla or Chengannur KSRTC Bus Station and alight at Kuriannoor Junction.

Auto-rickshaws and taxis are readily available from Kuriannoor Junction to Charalkunnu Mount (~2 km up the hill).
